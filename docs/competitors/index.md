## Suggested classification

- **Direct:** a plain white T-shirt is a central product and competes for the same use case.
- **Adjacent:** the shirt is part of a broader basics or underwear offer, or differs materially in price/use.
- **Reference:** useful for understanding a specific product, retail, or brand practice without being a direct rival.
