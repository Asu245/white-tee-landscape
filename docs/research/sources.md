# Source library

Collect reliable starting points here, then add sources as the research develops.

## Brand primary sources

- Official product pages, sizing charts, care instructions, return policies, and company/about pages.

## Customer evidence

- Product reviews and retailer reviews. Record the platform, date, sample context, and limits; do not generalize from a handful of reviews.

## Market context

- Prefer transparent research methods, clearly defined geography and category, and recent publication dates. Distinguish apparel-wide data from T-shirt-specific evidence.

## Source entry format

`[Title](URL) — publisher, source type, publication/check date, relevant scope, limitations.`
